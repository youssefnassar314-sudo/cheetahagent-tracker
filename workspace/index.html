            HOW IT WORKS
        

        
        

            

                

                

                    
                    1. Get the Template
                

                
Open the App Form panel and copy the raw template format.

            


            

                

                

                    
                    2. Fill & Check Boxes
                

                
Paste it in your notes. To check a box, put a character inside the parenthesis: (.) rent or (x) owned. Leave it blank to uncheck: ( ) others.

            


            

                

                

                    
                    3. Generate Document
                

                
Paste the completed format into the main box, select your Area, and click Generate.
